Xallakanm Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This policy explains what information the Xallakanm app ("the App"), made by Hamagardy, collects and how it is used. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Device code: a random ID created by the App to remember your settings and Pro status. It does not identify you personally.
  • Optional sign-in: if you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your name and email address. Sign-in is not required.
  • Username and suggestions: only if you choose to set a username or send us a suggestion.
  • Purchases: payments are handled by Apple. We only receive whether you have an active Pro plan. We never see your payment details.
  • Photos for the AI calculator: only the photo you choose is sent to an AI service to read the tiles. We do not keep the photo.
  • Live sharing: when you share a game or tournament, its team names and scores are stored online so others with the code can watch. We also count how many people are watching, using an anonymous random ID.
  • Basic usage and ads data: simple app usage statistics, and advertising identifiers used to show ads to free users.

We do not collect your contacts, location, or messages. Game scores you don't share stay on your device.

2. How we use it

  • To run the App's features (Pro access, live sharing, AI calculator, saved settings).
  • To show ads to free users.
  • To fix problems and improve the App.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Service providers

We use trusted providers only to run the App: Apple (sign-in and payments), Google (sign-in, Firebase, AdMob ads and the Gemini AI service), Supabase (database), and RevenueCat (checking Pro purchases). They process data only for these purposes and under their own privacy policies.

4. How long we keep it

  • Live shared games and tournaments are deleted automatically within 1 hour after they finish, or after 12 hours without activity.
  • Viewer counts are deleted within 10 minutes.
  • Account information is kept until you delete your account.

5. Your choices

  • You can use the App without signing in.
  • You can delete your account in the App's Settings, or by emailing us. This removes your account data.
  • You can limit ad tracking in your iPhone settings (Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).

6. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, but no online service can be completely secure.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

9. Contact

Questions or deletion requests: admin@hamagardy.com
